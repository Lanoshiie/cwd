What is it?

Diary of a CEO is a podcast where Steven Bartlett interviews the world's most successful people — entrepreneurs, scientists, athletes, and thinkers. Every episode will make you think differently about your life.

About Steven Bartlett

Steven Bartlett is a British entrepreneur who co-founded Social Chain and became the youngest Dragon on BBC's Dragons' Den at age 27. He is also the author of Happy Sexy Millionaire.
