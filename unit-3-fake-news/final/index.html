Scientists Confirm: Hot Cocoa on Rainy Days Proven to Cure Bad Moods

Researchers at the International Institute of Confectionery Wellness published landmark findings this week, confirming what chocolate lovers have known for centuries, a warm mug of cocoa on a grey, rainy afternoon is, in fact, the single most effective mood-lifting activity available to humankind. The study, which followed 3,000 participants across 14 countries, found that 99.8% reported feeling "significantly cozier" within minutes of their first sip.

"We are honestly a little embarrassed it took this long to prove,"said lead researcher Dr. Amélie Fondue. "The data could not be clearer. Hot cocoa works." World governments are expected to respond with new public hot cocoa initiatives by the end of the quarter.
